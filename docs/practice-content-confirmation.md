# Practice details for the patient guides

The new patient, insurance/payment, and urgent concern pages use the contact details and hours already published in this repository. They present unanswered operating questions as questions patients can ask, rather than as confirmed practice policies.

Before replacing those questions with specific answers, ask the practice to confirm:

| Topic | Details needed |
| --- | --- |
| Insurance | Exact participating plans/products; in-network versus out-of-network status; who verifies benefits; what information and channel patients should use. |
| Payment | Current payment methods; uninsured visit costs or estimates; payment timing; any financing/membership products actually offered. The existing SwipeSimple link is retained for paying existing bills. |
| First visit | Appointment length; arrival time; whether exam and cleaning are combined; required documents; approved secure intake/record-transfer instructions. |
| Families | How family appointments are coordinated; who to name as patient and contact; age-specific visit preparation the office approves. |
| Access | Parking, entrance, suite directions, wheelchair/stroller access, and any accessibility instructions. |
| Urgent concerns | Whether urgent appointments are offered; actual availability expectations; approved after-hours instructions and clinician-reviewed health guidance. |
| Follow-up | A response window the team can meet, follow-up channel, and what patients should do if a reply does not arrive. |

Do not publish plan participation, payment products, prices, specific visit lengths, same-day availability, after-hours contacts, or medical triage rules without confirmation. Existing published amenities are blankets, water, and entertainment during treatment.
